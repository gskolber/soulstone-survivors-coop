**Soulstone Coop 1.3**: map tasks anyone can start, partners' ascensions and passive objects shown to everyone, God meteors that hunt every player, a downed player you can actually find, and a fix for the Legionnaire / Demon Hunter bug that stopped everyone else's attacks.

**Everyone needs 1.3.0**: it does not connect to older versions. Install the same way: extract `SoulstoneCoop-1.3.0.zip` into the game folder over the previous version (Steam → right-click the game → Manage → Browse local files), then launch from Steam.

## New in 1.3

- **Fixed: a Legionnaire or Demon Hunter in the group stopped everyone else's attacks.** Rescuing a fallen centurion or capturing a demon made the other players stop casting for the rest of the map. Those summons are no longer replayed on the other machines; the units still show up there as before.
- **Map tasks for everyone:** the objective markers on the map (survive, kill the elites, kill the bosses) now appear for every player, and anyone can start one by standing on it, not just the host. More players on the marker start it faster. Everyone sees the task's timer or kill counter on their objectives panel.
- **Partners' ascensions are visible:** when a partner ascends or transforms (the Legionnaire after rescuing his centurions, and the other characters' ascended forms), you now see their new look and size.
- **Partners' passives are visible:** the objects of each character's own mechanic (fallen legionnaires, demonic binding stones, rifts, shards, shrines, altars, mana and soul crystals...) now appear for everyone. Only their owner can use them.
- **God meteors for everyone:** the God's meteors now take turns falling around every living player instead of only the host, and keep hunting the others while the host is down.
- **The host going down no longer stalls the fight:** enemies left standing around the downed host's body, with nobody alive nearby, are cleared, so the waves keep coming to the players still fighting.
- **Easier to find and revive a downed player:** the revive area is bigger (3.5 instead of 2.5) and drawn on the ground around the downed player, filling green as they're revived, and a line ties each downed player to the nearest living ally.
- **Enemies face the guest:** on a guest's screen, enemies close to a player now turn to face whoever they're fighting, instead of looking sideways.
- **Smoother hosting:** a performance pass on the work the mod does every frame (the enemy recycler check, enemy snapshots, puppet updates).

## Also new since 1.1 (1.2, not released on its own)

- **Chests and ore for everyone:** chests and ore deposits appear for every player, in the same place and with their minimap icon, and anyone can open them. The loot goes to everyone. Guarded chests spawn their guardians for everyone too.
- **Open them faster together:** each extra player standing on a chest, ore deposit or task marker speeds it up: 2 players 1.5× as fast, 3 players 2×, at most 3×. The host can change this with `[Coop] ChestOpenBonusPerPlayer` (`0` = same speed as alone).
- **Partners' rotating shields are visible**, and **channelled beams follow the aim**.
- **Regular enemies swing at guests:** guests see every enemy play its attack animation, not just bosses, elites and specials.
- **A more forgiving revive:** stepping away from a downed partner to dodge no longer throws the revive away: a full bar takes 30 seconds to drain.

Known limitations are listed in the [README](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop#known-limitations).
