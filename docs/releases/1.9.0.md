**Soulstone Coop 1.9** (in progress): Crusade mode: the lobby, the map changes, map tasks and the boss room.

**Everyone needs 1.9.0**: it does not connect to 1.8.0 or older (the run start carries the Crusade difficulty now). Everything in the [1.8.0 notes](1.8.0.md) is included.

## New

- **Crusade can be started in co-op:** the host's Fight on the Crusade tab brings everyone into the Crusade lobby, with the host's difficulty and dynamic level.
- **The host chooses the next map:** the map selection opens on the host's screen; guests see "The host is choosing the next map...". A guest walking into the lobby's portal (or a map's exit portal, or the goblin cave's) opens the selection on the host's screen.
- **Everyone goes to the host's map:** each player carries their own build, titan blood and inventory to the next map, as in Endless. The primordial portal and the goblin cave portal take everyone too.
- **The boss room** works for everyone: the guest's objectives follow the host's (key fragments, open the door, enter the room, kill the boss), the door opens and closes on every screen with its animation, everyone sees the boss presentation, and whoever is outside when the room closes is brought in. Standing on the door or the entrance counts for any player.
- **The lobby** is shared: any player standing on a titan blood rune fills it for everyone, and the unstable portal closes for everyone once the host's map selection starts.
- **Boss cutscenes** (the Crusade boss presentation, the Mastery main boss's intro, arena and death) hide the co-op overlays: party panel, partner name and health tags, downed rings and the level-up popup, which opens once the cutscene is over.
- **Map tasks on Crusade maps** show up for guests too. The ring a task puts up doesn't trap anyone in co-op: each player can run their own task or join another's. If a running task's area stays empty for 5 s in total (`[Coop] TaskAreaEmptySeconds`; the count pauses while someone is back inside), the task goes back to its marker and its enemies leave, with no loot.
- **Test keys** (`[Debug] TestKeys`): in Crusade, F7 on the host opens the map selection (in the lobby, on a map or in the goblin cave), without waiting for the portal; F8 on a map gives the host the key fragments and takes everyone to the boss room door.

## Known limitations

- After the map's boss dies, the titan blood and the exit portals are still the host's; the goblin cave's chests and the final boss aren't shared yet (docs/cruzada-investigacao.md, steps 2 to 4).
- Restart on the end screen isn't followed in Crusade: go back to the menu and Fight again.
