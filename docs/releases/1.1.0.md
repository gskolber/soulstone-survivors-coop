**Soulstone Coop 1.1**: a party panel to follow your partners, an upgrade panel the HUD no longer covers, gamepad support for it, and fixes for missing enemies and curses on guests.

**Everyone needs 1.1.0**: it does not connect to 1.0.x. Install the same way: extract `SoulstoneCoop-1.1.0.zip` into the game folder over the previous version (Steam → right-click the game → Manage → Browse local files), then launch from Steam.

## New in 1.1

- **Party panel** ([#7](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop/issues/7)): at the top left of the run, one row per partner with their character, Steam name, health, level, XP to their next level, Death Guard charges left, and whether they're down, being revived, dead or disconnected. Turn it off with `[Coop] PartyHud = false`.

  <sub>Mock-up: one real partner plus two sample rows drawn by the panel.</sub>

- **Upgrade panel clear of the HUD** ([#5](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop/issues/5)): the compact upgrade panel now sits at the top of the screen, never under the health bar or skill slots, on 16:9 and ultrawide. `[Coop] LevelUpPanelPosition = Bottom` puts it just above the health bar instead. If you had changed its position before, set it again: the old setting is no longer read.

- **Gamepad on the upgrade panel:** D-pad left/right picks a card, the game's confirm button takes it, and reroll, banish and lock use the buttons shown on the panel. D-pad down hides the panel so you can play. The keyboard shortcuts (R, F, C) work again too.
- **Guests see every enemy** ([#6](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop/issues/6)): each machine rolls its own set of enemy types per run, so guests sometimes didn't see the regular mobs for the first minutes. The host now tells guests which enemy to show.
- **Curses follow the host** ([#3](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop/issues/3)): guests now play with the host's curses instead of their own, so the enemies and objectives match.

Known limitations are listed in the [README](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop#known-limitations).
