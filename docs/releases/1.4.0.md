**Soulstone Coop 1.4.0**: come back to a run with the build you had, plus everything from 1.3.1 and a round of fixes and speed-ups.

**Everyone needs 1.4.0**: it does not connect to 1.3.x or older. Install the same way: extract `SoulstoneCoop-1.4.0.zip` into the game folder over the previous version, then launch from Steam. Everything in the [1.3.1 notes](1.3.1.md) and the [1.3.0 notes](1.3.0.md) is included.

## New in 1.4.0

- **Rejoin with your build (experimental):** with `[Coop] AllowRejoin = true` on the host, a player who drops out of a run and reconnects comes back with the level, upgrades, runes and items they had, instead of as a new character. Up to the last 5 seconds of progress can be lost, and closing the game loses the build.
- **Your character's voice on chests and ore:** guests now hear their own character's "opening a chest" or "mining" line when they step on one.
- **Smoother with missing assets:** when a partner's summon, passive object, ascension model or an enemy can't be found in your game, it's no longer searched for again on every spawn (that caused small hitches).
- **Fixed:** a partner changing skin while ascended no longer keeps the old ascension model on; the Legionnaire's ascension piece is found even when his skill tree loads late; small memory leak in the passive object markers.

Known limitations are listed in the [README](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop#known-limitations).
