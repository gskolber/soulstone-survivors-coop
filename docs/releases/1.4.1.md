**Soulstone Coop 1.4.1**: fixes from the 1.4.0 test.

**Everyone needs 1.4.1**: the handshake refuses other versions. Everything in the [1.4.0 notes](1.4.0.md) is included.

## Fixed

- **Enemies walking backwards:** an enemy on the move now always faces where it's going on every player's screen. Before, an enemy that had just attacked someone kept looking at that player (often the host) while walking toward another.
- **Death taking small steps:** enemies on the move now walk at the host's speed without stopping at each update, so slow walkers like Death move smoothly on every player's screen.
- **Other characters' mini-game areas:** the Demon Hunter's binding area (and the other characters' passive objects) now show with the game's own model and effects for the other players, loaded when first needed. If it still can't be loaded, the stand-in ring now matches the real size of the area.

Known limitations are listed in the [README](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop#known-limitations).
