**Soulstone Coop 1.6.0**: get back into the match, and more of your partners' heroes on your screen.

**Everyone needs 1.6.0**: it does not connect to 1.5.x or older. Everything in the [1.5.0 notes](1.5.0.md) and earlier is included.

## New

- **Back into the match:** a player who goes back to the menu (for example after surrendering) presses **Fight** to rejoin the host's run, and comes back with their build if it's the same run. A player whose connection drops reconnects by itself for up to 45 seconds and carries on in the same run, without reloading. `[Coop] AllowRejoin` is now on by default (an existing config keeps its old value: set it to `true` on the host).
- **Animations on partners' mini-game objects:** the Pirate's first mate digs, the treasure chest opens, the Shaman's ritualists dance.
- **Downed players lie on the ground** on every screen.

## Known limitations

- The Machinist's rotating crystals still don't show on partners' screens.
- If the host moves to the next stage or restarts while a player is reconnecting, that player comes back into the old stage.
