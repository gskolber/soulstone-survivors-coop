**Soulstone Coop 1.3.1**: partners' ascensions you can actually see, with their effects, plus fixes from the 1.3.0 test.

Never published on its own: it ships inside [1.4.0](1.4.0.md). Everything in the [1.3.0 notes](1.3.0.md) is included.

## New in 1.3.1

- **Ascensions really show now:** the Legionnaire's ascension grows his model and adds its extra piece on everyone's screen, and the legionnaires he summons (and the Demon Hunter's creatures, the chaos golem...) are visible to the others again.
- **Ascension effects for everyone:** the activation flash, the sound and the world light change of a partner's ascension now play on your screen too.
- **Partners' mod skins:** if you have the same mod skin installed, you see your partner wearing it (before, everyone saw the default skin).
- **Weapon companions:** the companions some weapons add now show up for the other players.
- **Passive objects you can't load:** when another character's passive object isn't available in your game, a colored marker shows where it is.
- **Fixed: enemies moonwalking and Death crawling.** Enemies only turn to face a player while they stand still; on the move they face where they go again, and Death moves at its normal speed on the guests' screens.

Known limitations are listed in the [README](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop#known-limitations).
