**Soulstone Coop 1.7**: chests, ore and map tasks for every player, your partners' ascensions and hero mechanics on your screen, a revive you can actually pull off, reconnecting mid-run, and dash dodge for guests. Most of this release was contributed by [@AndrinoC](https://github.com/AndrinoC) ([#9](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop/pull/9)).

**Everyone needs 1.7.0**: it does not connect to 1.1.x. Install the same way: extract `SoulstoneCoop-1.7.0.zip` into the game folder over the previous version (Steam → right-click the game → Manage → Browse local files), then launch from Steam.

## New in 1.7

- **Chests and ore for everyone:** chests and ore deposits appear for every player, in the same place and with their minimap icon, and anyone can open them, not just the host. The loot goes to everyone. Guarded chests spawn their guardians for everyone too.
- **Map tasks for everyone:** the objective markers (survive, kill the elites, kill the bosses) appear for every player and anyone can start one. Everyone sees the task's timer or kill counter.
- **Faster together:** each extra player standing on a chest, ore deposit or task marker speeds it up: 2 players 1.5× as fast, 3 players 2×, at most 3×. The host can change this with `[Coop] ChestOpenBonusPerPlayer` (`0` = same speed as alone).
- **Partners' heroes, as they really are:** their ascensions (new look, size, flash, sound and light), their mini-game objects (fallen legionnaires, binding stones, the Pirate's ghost ship, the Shaman's pyre, the Machinist's minerals, Wukong's orbs...) with their progress bars and animations, their rotating shields, and channelled beams that follow their aim. All of it is visual on your side; damage is counted where it really happens.
- **Partners' mod skins:** if you have the same mod skin installed, you see your partner wearing it.
- **A revive you can pull off:** stepping away to dodge no longer throws the revive away (a full bar takes 30 seconds to drain), the revive area is bigger and drawn on the ground, and a downed player lies on the ground with a red pillar and a line to the nearest living ally.
- **Back into the match:** a player whose connection drops reconnects by itself for up to 45 seconds and carries on in the same run with their build. A player who went back to the menu presses **Fight** to rejoin the host's run (as a new character). `[Coop] AllowRejoin` is now on by default for new installs; an existing config keeps its old value.
- **Dash dodge for guests:** a guest who dashes through an attack dodges it like the host does.
- **Enemies on the guest's screen:** regular enemies play their attack animations, face whoever they're fighting, and walk at the host's speed (no more moonwalking or a crawling Death). God meteors hunt every player, and enemies left around a downed host's body are cleared so the waves keep coming.

## Fixed

- **A Legionnaire or Demon Hunter in the group stopped everyone else's attacks.**
- **The host no longer pauses because of a guest** rejoining or dropping mid-run.
- **The mod's test keys no longer work in normal play** (Insert, Delete and PageDown even worked in solo games). They now need `[Debug] TestKeys = true`.
- **Sturdier against bad packets:** a malformed or oversized message from a peer is dropped instead of affecting everyone's game.

## Known limitations

- A player who surrenders and rejoins with Fight comes back as a new character.
- A guest's "on dash dodge" upgrades don't trigger yet.
- The Machinist's rotating crystals don't show on partners' screens yet.

The full list is in the [README](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop#known-limitations).
