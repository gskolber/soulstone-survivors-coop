**Soulstone Coop 1.6.2**: the test keys are off.

**Everyone needs 1.6.2**: it does not connect to 1.6.1 or older. Everything in the [1.6.1 notes](1.6.1.md) is included.

## Fixed

- **The mod's test keys no longer work in normal play.** F1 (drop the connection), F2, F3, F5-F11 (levels, chests, portals, victory, revive, lethal hit...) worked in every co-op session, and Insert, Delete and PageDown even in solo games (start hosting, connect to 127.0.0.1, spawn a test copy of your character). They now need `[Debug] TestKeys = true`. F4 (host lifts the others' pause) and Tab (hide the level-up panel) still work as before.

## Known limitations

- If the host takes a portal, restarts or goes back to the menu while a guest is disconnected, the guest reconnects into the old stage. Surrender and press Fight to join the host's current run.
- A player who surrenders and rejoins with Fight comes back as a new character.
- A guest's "on dash dodge" upgrades don't trigger yet.
- The Machinist's rotating crystals still don't show on partners' screens.
