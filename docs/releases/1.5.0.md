**Soulstone Coop 1.5.0**: every hero's mini-game shows for the other players.

**Everyone needs 1.5.0**: it does not connect to 1.4.x or older. Everything in the [1.4.1 notes](1.4.1.md) and earlier is included.

## New

- **Every hero's mini-game objects for the other players:** the Pirate's ghost ship, its wheel and first mate, the Shaman's ritual pyre, the Machinist's minerals and effects, and any other hero's objects now appear on everyone's screen, found automatically instead of from a fixed list.
- **Progress bars and fades:** the Demon Hunter's capture bar, mining and digging progress, and objects fading in or out now move on the other players' screens too.

## Fixed (since 1.4.1)

- **Downed players** stay on the ground instead of sinking under the map, so the revive ring, the new red pillar and the guide line are always visible, and the revive no longer leaves them underground.
- A partner's skill that can't be replayed on your screen no longer floods the log with errors.

## Known limitations

- Animations on those objects (the first mate digging, the ritualists dancing) aren't shown; the objects stay in their default pose.
- The Machinist's rotating crystals don't show on partners yet.
