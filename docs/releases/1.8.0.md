**Soulstone Coop 1.8**: Mastery mode in co-op.

**Everyone needs 1.8.0**: it does not connect to 1.7.0 or older. Everything in the [1.7.0 notes](1.7.0.md) is included.

## New

- **Mastery in co-op:** the host's Mastery run now plays out the same way for everyone.
  - The main boss and the pillars show up for every player.
  - The rune is shared: when it fills (the host or any guest standing on it), everyone gets the cycle's ascension or reset at the same time, like in single player, and the lords come for everyone.
  - Cycles, pillars breaking, the victory and the Home portal follow the host.
  - The main boss fight plays out for everyone: his intro, the pull into the void arena, his death cutscene.
- **Test keys** (`[Debug] TestKeys`): in Mastery, F7 on the host jumps to the main boss fight and starts its second part a few seconds in; F8 ends the run with the victory.

## Known limitations

- Played on one PC (two game windows) so far, not yet over Steam.
- The rune goes off on the host's side, a moment after it fills on a guest's screen.
- In the main boss's arena, each player only sees their own channeling effect on the boss.
