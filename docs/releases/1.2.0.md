**Soulstone Coop 1.2**: chests and ore for every player, partners' rotating shields and beams shown as they really are, enemies that swing at guests, and a more forgiving revive.

**Everyone needs 1.2.0**: it does not connect to 1.1.x. Install the same way: extract `SoulstoneCoop-1.2.0.zip` into the game folder over the previous version (Steam → right-click the game → Manage → Browse local files), then launch from Steam.

## New in 1.2

- **Chests and ore for everyone:** chests and ore deposits now appear for every player, in the same place and with their minimap icon, and anyone can open them, not just the host. The loot goes to everyone, as before. Guarded chests spawn their guardians for everyone too.
- **Open them faster together:** each extra player standing on a chest or ore deposit speeds it up: 2 players open it 1.5× as fast, 3 players 2×, at most 3×. The host can change this with `[Coop] ChestOpenBonusPerPlayer` (`0` = same speed as alone).
- **Partners' rotating shields are visible:** shields that orbit a partner now spin around them on your screen too. Like the rest of a partner's skills, they're only a picture on your side; their damage is counted where it really happens.
- **Channelled beams follow the aim:** a partner's beams and other channelled skills now follow where they're aiming, instead of staying pointed where the cast started.
- **Regular enemies swing at guests:** guests now see every enemy play its attack animation, not just bosses, elites and specials.
- **A more forgiving revive:** stepping away from a downed partner to dodge no longer throws the revive away. The bar now drains slowly (a full bar takes 30 seconds to empty) instead of emptying in a second and a half.

Known limitations are listed in the [README](https://github.com/gskolber/soulstone-survivors-coop#known-limitations).
